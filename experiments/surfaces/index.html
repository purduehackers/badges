WHAT THIS EXPERIMENT TELLS YOU

Compare appearance.
Keep the physical proof.

The stamp models ink coverage, uneven contact, paper grain, and pressure-driven edge spread. It doesn’t predict exact results from your ink pad or rubber.

The acrylic uses one surface with separate glossy and matte regions. Fingerprints affect reflection roughness. Scratches affect surface normals, not the design’s color.

Textures: ambientCG Paper 001, Fingerprints 002, and TextureCan Plastic 0002. All CC0. Texture scale and strength are estimates.
